   The script uses a recursive XOR cipher. It applies a new "layer" of 
   XOR transformations at every step 'n'. 
   - Layer 0 XORs indices [0...end]
   - Layer 1 XORs indices [1...end]
   - Layer 2 XORs indices [2...end]

   the third three times, and so on.

   Since (A ^ B) ^ B = A, the XOR operation is its own inverse. 
   To decrypt, I simply had to apply the same XOR operations but 
   in the EXACT REVERSE order of the encryption (from the last 
   recursive step back to the first).
