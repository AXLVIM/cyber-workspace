#1. Intro
EtherChannel (specifically Link Aggregation) allows us to bundle multiple physical links into a single logical interface. In your previous task, if the cable between f1/0 and f1/0 failed, the whole network died.
By adding a second cable and configuring EtherChannel, we gain redundancy (if one cable breaks, the other stays up) and increased bandwidth.

#2. Summary
Physical Layer: Add a second cable between SW1 and SW2 (using f1/3 to f1/3).

Logical Layer: Create a Port-channel interface.

Protocol: We will use PAgP (Cisco proprietary) or LACP (Standard). For this Cisco image, the "On" mode or PAgP is most common.

Trunking: We move the Trunk configuration from the physical ports to the new Port-channel interface.

#3. Solution
#Step A: GNS3 Topology Update
Add a Link: Connect SW1 (f1/3) to SW2 (f1/3).

You now have two cables connecting the switches: f1/0 to f1/0 and f1/3 to f1/3.
###Warning: Fix f1/0 to f1/2. like this: f1/2 to f1/2 and f1/3 to f1/3. Now you're good to go bro

#Step B: Switch Configuration (SW1 and SW2)
You must perform these steps on both switches. It is critical that the settings match on both sides.

1. Group the interfaces into a Channel:

#Bash

SW1# conf t
SW1(config)# interface range fastEthernet 1/0 , fastEthernet 1/3
SW1(config-if-range)# channel-group 1 mode on
SW1(config-if-range)# exit
Note: "Mode on" forces the EtherChannel without negotiating. It's the most stable for the 3745 image in GNS3.

2. Configure the Logical "Port-Channel" as a Trunk:
Now that the two wires are one "team" (Port-channel 1), we apply the Trunk settings to the team, not the individual wires.

#Bash

SW1(config)# interface port-channel 1
SW1(config-if)# switchport trunk encapsulation dot1q
SW1(config-if)# switchport mode trunk
SW1(config-if)# no shutdown
SW1(config-if)# exit
Step C: Verification
Wait about 30 seconds for Spanning Tree to recalculate, then run these commands:

Check EtherChannel Status:

#Bash

SW1# show etherchannel summary
Look for (P) next to the ports. P means "Bundled in port-channel." If you see (D), the link is down.

Check Trunk Status:

Bash

SW1# show interface trunk
The "Port" column should now say Po1 instead of Fa1/0.
